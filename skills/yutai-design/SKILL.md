# Yutai design

Use the supplied identity without confusing product rules, demonstrated behaviour and proposed presentation art direction. This project-scoped package includes the user-supplied brand kit and a dated product-design reference. It is not a global installation or an approval of every specimen in the visual walkthrough.

## Start with the surface

Read [brand-kit routing and usage](references/brand-kit.md) for current general identity, logo selection, sizes and the conditional Benefits badge. Read [foundations](references/foundations.md) when reproducing a product zone and [claim boundaries](references/claim-boundaries.md) before business-facing copy. [tokens.json](references/tokens.json) keeps general brand, shared linen and product V2 values separate; [font faces](assets/yutai-fonts.css) and [asset provenance](assets/provenance.json) are bundled.

- Product specimen: follow that zone's current rules. Holder H2 and Admin A2 override only the older rules they name. Docs retain the shared linen/Patina theme. Studio and public-site styling are separate scopes.
- Communication artifact: use the new supplied kit for general identity, then design the composition for the message. Preserve selected layouts, palette use and paragraph typography. Product CSS or a brand palette is not a mandate for a full navy canvas, dashboard chrome or repeated card grid.
- Proposed change: show it separately from the source baseline. A walkthrough edit or attractive mock-up is not a revised product contract.

## Identity that carries across

- Default general communication mark: [new horizontal ink](assets/brand-kit/svg/yutai-lockup-horizontal-ink.svg) on light; [new horizontal on-dark](assets/brand-kit/svg/yutai-lockup-horizontal-on-dark.svg) on dark; [new horizontal white](assets/brand-kit/svg/yutai-lockup-horizontal-white.svg) on photos/coloured backgrounds after contrast checking. Use the [routing table](references/brand-kit.md) for stacked, seal, wordmark, avatar and badge variants. Old `assets/logos/` files are historical product references. Never type, redraw, retint or stretch artwork.
- The kit names Sora for headlines/UI and DM Mono for code/labels. Keep Source Sans 3 for current presentation paragraphs and explicit product-zone reading text; the kit does not explicitly ban it. DM Mono is not the general paragraph face. Noto Sans JP is specified for Japanese but is **not bundled**; supplied SVG Japanese is outlined and needs no font.
- Use the loaded weights: Sora 600/700, Source Sans 3 400/600, DM Mono 400/500. Do not ask a browser to invent a missing weight.
- Write **Yutai** in prose; the supplied outlined wordmark is lowercase **yutai**. Sentence case, British English, direct labels and tabular figures remain the communication conventions. Status also needs words or shape.
- In V2 product specimens green means **earned** for holders and **live** for operators. Ink is the primary action. Green is not a generic heading, navigation state or decorative call-to-action colour.
- Light green product marks need their contrasting edge. A ready ticket's edge follows its own painted company fill/ink, even inside a dark preview. Fixed logo colours are artwork, not interface state or CTA tokens.
- Keep clear space of at least one quarter of the rendered seal height. Minimum seal 16px / 6mm; horizontal lockup 80px / 24mm; Benefits badge 24px tall. Do not crop or retype the stacked Japanese artwork.
- Use marks accurately, with no implied partnership/endorsement absent written authority and no incorporation into another product/company/domain name. The kit-provided company name and technology-provider positioning are attributed brand information, not registry, licensing or regulatory verification.

## Objects must retain their meaning

- Holder benefit: ticket with a **bottom** stub, not a side action column. Only ready tickets receive the company's full colour; other states remain neutral.
- Operator worklist: evidence body plus a **right-side** action stub. Do not transplant it into the holder ticket.
- Company: notched square pass. Person/account: neutral circle. A chain is not a company pass.
- Holding timelines share a day scale while each programme keeps its own tier marks. No common tier line across different programmes; unknown next-tier timing is not top-tier attainment.
- Read the exact geometry and type in [foundations](references/foundations.md) when reproducing those objects. Scale an authentic specimen as a unit when placing it in a slide.

## Benefits badge is conditional

Place “Benefits by yutai” next to an instrument only with **at least one verified live benefit**, a link to its details, and the same terms for every eligible holder. It must not encourage acquiring a security. Do not use it next to fictional instruments or illustrative deck offers by default. A catalogue or simulated/live source label is not proof. The [brand-kit reference](references/brand-kit.md) gives the artwork routes and evidence boundary.

## Executive presentations

For the current Fortune 500 deck, preserve the settled brief: evaluate Yutai as a commercial partner; broader holder-membership ambition with tokenised holdings first; intended model with one dedicated readiness slide; business-literate, nontechnical audience; 10–15 visual slides.

Before extending a new full-deck direction, put actual rendered compositions in front of Max. A prose outline approves a story, not unseen colours, imagery or layouts. Reuse a visually selected direction; do not repeat the rejected deck as a brand template.

Use a distinct composition for each communication job: an opening idea, an issuer/asset/holder relationship, a tangible benefit experience, programme controls, or a commercial decision. Repeating one title/summary/diagram/footer frame does not create visual pacing.

Imagery must explain the product or the experience on that page. A cinema crowd is not a company-level metaphor for Yutai. Product specimens, clear mechanism diagrams and relevant experience imagery may each lead where they earn it. Label hypothetical programmes and synthetic product views honestly.

Keep detailed source and legal distinctions in notes and the readiness discussion where possible; never remove a qualification whose absence makes the main claim false. Do not turn every slide into caveat text.

The full deck retains Max's requirement for at least ten substantive review passes. Count actual artifact reviews with findings and dispositions, not research inputs or ten repetitions of the same check. Technical checks establish rendering and controls; they cannot establish Max's visual acceptance.

## Finish at the right claim

Inspect rendered pages at their delivery sizes and verify meaningful interactions/export when present. Review source fidelity, commercial logic and visual clarity separately. Revisit only changed material or actual unresolved findings. Report what is built, what is delivered, and what remains unverified.

This package conveys no authority to publish, alter the original product repository, grant access or assert product readiness. Existing user authorisations and the active project ledger govern those actions; do not invent another approval flow.
