## Settled executive framing

Audience: Fortune 500 executives, business literate and nontechnical. Decision sought: evaluate Yutai as a commercial partner. Present the intended end-state with one dedicated readiness slide. Lead with the broader stock-holder membership opportunity and name tokenised holdings as the entry point; conventional brokerage and registered-shareholder routes remain expansion work unless current evidence establishes them.

The working product model concerns recognition, tenure/eligibility and voluntary benefits around supported holdings. It is not evidence of universal stock coverage, a broker/custodian role, a formal shareholder register, statutory voting or investment advice. Use **holder** unless the exact instrument and legal position establish shareholder rights.

## Preserve these distinctions

| Visual or sentence | Evidence needed before making it factual |
|---|---|
| A corporate issuer recognises a token wrapper | Actual programme authority; corporate issuer and token issuer are distinct roles |
| A token or account maps to a security | Instrument/wrapper mapping and effective source evidence, not a generic ticker match |
| “Days held” or a tier | Programme policy, quantity/history/source quality; not a tax holding period or statutory entitlement |
| A brand benefit is available | Sponsor/brand permission, funded capacity, inventory, participant terms and fulfilment evidence |
| A claim completes | Actual end-to-end authorisation and delivery/participation receipt, not a seeded offer or mock screen |
| A distributor integration is live | The relevant supported account/proof flow, not merely an SDK, adapter or named provider |
| An issuer can self-serve | A working permitted operator/issuer journey; Studio design/source presence alone is insufficient |
| A programme improves retention or ROI | Measured commercial results; these remain hypotheses when untested |

Connect is separately consented customer/purchase context; it does not mean payment-card issuance. Voice is nonbinding feedback, not statutory voting. Affinity and partner catalogue relationships do not establish signed partnerships. Markets transfers are not automatically executed trades, and addresses are not unique people.

## Copy and diagram practice

An issuer, distributor, token issuer, evidence provider, programme operator, benefit funder and fulfiller can be different actors. A company may hold several roles; do not merge them by drawing one ambiguous arrow. Label evidence flows, decisions, money/capacity and benefit delivery according to what each connection means.

Hypothetical companies, quantities, tier thresholds and experiences must be clearly illustrative. Product specimens are designed examples unless they are actual screenshots with a verified context. Never turn an illustrative Nike/Netflix example into a client logo or imply an existing sponsored programme.

Keep supporting source anchors and caveats in presenter notes. The single readiness slide should distinguish reviewed foundation, partnership validation and expansion. That structure does not permit a false claim elsewhere in the deck. Unsupported “first”, “only”, guaranteed outcomes, prices, universal coverage, legal clearance and signed customer claims stay out.

## Supplied-kit usage constraints

The kit names Yutai Technologies, Inc. and positions Yutai as a technology provider rather than issuer, broker, exchange or custodian. Attribute that to the supplied kit; do not call it independent corporate or licensing verification. Its voluntary issuer/trading-venue benefits are distinct from statutory shareholder rights. No mark may imply an endorsement or partnership without written authority.

The distributor “Benefits by yutai” badge is an instrument-level live-programme signal. Before use require at least one verified live benefit, a working benefit-details link, and equal terms for all eligible holders; no security-acquisition nudge. Seeded offers, source-mode labels and hypothetical deck programmes do not qualify. Omit the badge from fictional instrument examples by default. Ordinary Yutai attribution uses the general logo without implying a live benefit.
